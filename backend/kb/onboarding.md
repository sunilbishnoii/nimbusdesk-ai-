# Onboarding and demos
## Setup time
Most teams are fully set up in one to three days. Enterprise implementations usually take two to four weeks because of single sign-on and data migration.
## Migration
Tickets can be imported from Zendesk or Freshdesk using a free CSV import tool. The import usually completes within a few hours.
## Training
Growth customers receive two live training sessions. Enterprise customers receive unlimited training sessions and a dedicated onboarding manager.
## Product demo
A 30-minute personalised product demo can be booked with the sales team on weekdays. Please share your team size and main requirements so the demo can be tailored.
