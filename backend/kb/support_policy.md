# Support and service levels
## Support channels and response times
Starter customers receive email support with a reply within 24 hours on business days. Growth customers receive priority email and chat support with a first response within 4 hours. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 phone support with a first response within 1 hour for critical issues.
## Uptime
The uptime commitment is 99.9% for Growth and 99.95% for Enterprise. Live service status is published on the public status page.
## Escalation
Billing disputes and complaints are escalated to a human account manager within one business day.
