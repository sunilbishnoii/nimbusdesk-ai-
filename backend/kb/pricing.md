# Pricing
## Plans
NimbusDesk offers three plans: Starter, Growth and Enterprise. The Starter plan costs $15 per agent per month and includes the shared inbox and live chat widget. The Growth plan costs $39 per agent per month and adds ticket automation, analytics dashboard and priority support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager and a custom service level agreement.
## Billing and discounts
Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Nonprofits and educational institutions receive 30% off any plan. Invoices can be issued in USD, INR or EUR. Accepted payment methods are credit card, bank transfer for annual plans, and UPI for customers in India.
## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start a trial. A trial can be extended once by 7 days on request to the sales team.
## Refunds and cancellation
Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable but can be cancelled at any time from the billing page.
