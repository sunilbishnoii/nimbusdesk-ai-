Lead labelling tool

For each customer enquiry, decide how valuable it is as a sales lead. Click a button or press H / W / C. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.
How to decide (read once)
Choose this when…Examples
HOTWants to buy or book a demo and there is a near-term time or clear commitment. Sales should call today."Demo this week, 30 agents, budget approved" · "Send the contract, we sign this month"
WARMA genuine prospect, but just researching, no deadline, or missing details."What does the Growth plan cost?" · "We might switch next quarter" · "Do you integrate with Slack?"
COLDNo chance of a new sale: existing customer with a problem or cancelling, complaint, job seeker, spam, student, greeting, off-topic, gibberish."I can't log in" · "I want to cancel" · "tell me a joke" · "We offer SEO services"

Rules: judge only the text. Pricing/feature question with no timeline = WARM. Existing customer with a problem = COLD. Torn between two? Pick the LOWER one.
